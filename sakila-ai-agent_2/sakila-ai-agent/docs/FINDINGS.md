## Conclusion supported by the data

1. **Customers return** (all of them) and **explore widely** (median 13 of 16 categories; >93% of next rentals switch).
2. **Retention ≠ category loyalty** is confirmed — but the switching rate is almost exactly what random choice across 16 evenly-demanded categories would produce.
3. **Where customers go next is not predictable from the previous category** in Sakila (χ² independence not rejected, negligible Cramér's V, hold-out accuracy ≈ random). No gateway category is supported.
4. **Business implication:** instead of betting on one 'next genre', Sakila should support discovery — cross-category suggestions, rotating shelves, and movie-level ranking by popularity + availability + novelty to the customer.
